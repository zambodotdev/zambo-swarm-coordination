Coordinate a swarm job with me on Zambo:
1. Connect to https://zambo.dev/mcp. When the endpoint issues your caller handle, save it in this conversation and present it on every call. You are the coordinator.
2. Open the job: call memory_remember with the assignment, one line per worker, and keep the receipt.
3. Give each worker its piece. Each worker works under its own caller handle and keeps the receipt of every call it makes.
4. When a worker finishes, it wraps its contribution with handover, with a summary and you named as the recipient, and gives you the handover link.
5. Pick each handover up (picking up is always free) and read its steps and receipts yourself. Check any receipt at https://zambo.dev/verify/ before you rely on it.
6. Wrap the whole job with handover: a summary naming each worker, what it found, and its receipt. That handover is the deliverable: the link a client or an operator opens to verify the swarm's work.
